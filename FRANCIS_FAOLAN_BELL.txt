   **2. Your favourite sport and why you like it:**

    I don't think of it as a sport, but in terms of movement: I love dancing. Mainly jazz (lindy hop, charleston) and blues. Or just generally throwing myself around a dancefloor. 
    
    I like dance because you can immediately connect to people without language barriers, and you do not need to 'get to know' them before any intimacy can be established. 
    
    In dance, connection, or 'getting to know' someone is immediately already intimate (1) - you are physically close to each other and must speak/listen with strong intention/attention for the conversation to function. And so it is much easier to determine whether you align with each other, because what someone values on the dancefloor can tell you a lot about their values outside of it. (Much more than the ostensible personality markers of someone's work or hobbies.)
    
    (1) Thinking of "intimacy" in relation to its roots - from Late Latin intimatus, the past participle of intimare "make known, announce, impress," and from Latin intimus "inmost, innermost, deepest" (adj.) (etymonline.com)
    
    Also, because dance has transformed how I view mistakes - without any inherent negativity but instead as opportunities to learn, become more adaptable, and create something new.
